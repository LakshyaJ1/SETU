# Metrics PDF surface brief

Mode: Read. Audience: SETU engineers and demo reviewers deciding which results
are trustworthy, rather than prospective users being asked to trust a release.
The Android daylight identity is the visual authority; the older dark HTML
report remains untouched. This extends an established identity, not a rebrand.

THESIS: distinguish a useful estimator from a demonstrated navigation solution.
Show the failed conditions as prominently as successes; reject a promotional
cover made of headline accuracy numbers.

OWN-WORLD: forest-green typography, daylight-neutral paper, native-like sans,
blue measured-series ink, quiet rules and generous margins. No decorative maps,
fake trajectories, photographic assets or unsourced performance claims.

STORY: read the verdict, inspect all algorithm/condition combinations, understand
the separate real-ride and speed-model evidence, then identify release blockers.

FIRST PAGE: SETU and an explicit evaluation-only label; a large decision heading;
the agreed target; the actual all-profile 10-second comparison; a short conclusion.

FORM: a daylight engineering dossier, shaped directly around the requested
algorithm comparison. Fixed A4 pages with vector charts and selectable text.
No concept tournament or image generation is needed for this narrow data artifact.

Evidence is generated from the hash-validated road-stress matrix and actual model
manifest. The three selected rides are diagnostic data, never a fabricated
independent field benchmark. Missing results remain visible. The report is a
current research snapshot, not the final release certificate.
